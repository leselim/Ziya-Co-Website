Our story

Jewellery that carries meaning. Beauty that begins within

Inspired by the meaning associated with light, radiance and brilliance, Ziya & Co. was created around a simple belief: jewellery is more than something we wear. It reflects who we are, what we cherish and the moments that shape our lives.

We believe the most meaningful pieces become part of our stories. They celebrate becoming, hold memories close and remind us of the beauty found in life’s everyday moments.

So we begin quietly. With considered design, restraint, and an eye for the detail that only shows itself once a piece has been worn for a while.
