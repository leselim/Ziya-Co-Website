Our story

Why should that feeling be reserved for special occasions?

At ZIYA & CO, jewellery has always been more than an accessory. There is something about the perfect pair of earrings, a delicate bracelet, a necklace that somehow makes you feel a little more beautiful, a little more confident, and completely yourself.

ZIYA & CO believes jewellery is meant to be lived in. Worn on ordinary days and on big ones, on days you are dressed up and days you are simply running errands. Because you deserve to feel beautiful every day, not only when there is somewhere special to go.

Every piece is chosen with the intention of adding a little something to your day. A little sparkle, a little confidence, a little joy.

Because sometimes, the smallest details make you feel the most special

The philosophy

Jewellery is meant to be lived in

The hope is simple. That when you put on your ZIYA & CO jewellery, you feel amazing. You feel beautiful. You feel like you, just a little more elevated.

Good to know

Jewellery meant to be worn, not put away

A few things worth knowing before the collection opens. Material details for each piece will be listed on its own product page.

  • 01

    Made for everyday wear

    Designed with everyday wear in mind, and made to be resistant to tarnishing. Like all jewellery, proper care helps maintain the finish and keeps pieces looking beautiful for longer.

  • 02

    Ready for real life

    Designed to handle everyday life, including occasional exposure to water. Best kept away from prolonged water, swimming pools, the ocean and harsh chemicals.

  • 03

    Worn, loved, enjoyed

    Not saved for a special occasion. Pieces are made to be worn, and a little care goes a long way: a soft dry cloth after wearing, stored cool and dry.
