My story

Why should that feeling be reserved for special occasions?

ZIYA & CO was created from a place of love — a deep, undeniable love for jewellery and the way it makes you feel. For me, jewellery has always been more than just an accessory. There’s something about putting on that perfect pair of earrings, slipping on a delicate bracelet or adding a necklace that somehow makes you feel a little more beautiful, a little more confident, and completely yourself. And I wanted to share that feeling.

I believe jewellery is meant to be lived in. To be worn on ordinary days, on big days, on days when you’re dressed up and days when you’re simply running errands. Because you deserve to feel beautiful every day — not just when there’s somewhere special to go.

Every piece is chosen with the intention of adding a little something to your day — a little sparkle, a little confidence, a little joy.

Because sometimes, the smallest details can make you feel the most special

Good to know

Made to be worn, not saved

A few things I’d like you to know before opening day.

Are Ziya & Co pieces tarnish-resistant?

My jewellery is designed with everyday wear in mind and is made to be resistant to tarnishing. However, like all jewellery, proper care will help maintain its finish and keep your pieces looking beautiful for longer.

Is Ziya & Co jewellery waterproof?

My pieces are designed to handle everyday life, including occasional exposure to water. However, I recommend keeping your jewellery away from prolonged exposure to water, swimming pools, the ocean and harsh chemicals to preserve its finish.

Can I wear my jewellery every day?

Absolutely. That’s what Ziya & Co is all about. My jewellery is designed to be worn, loved and enjoyed every day — not saved for a special occasion.

Will my jewellery change colour?

With proper care, your jewellery should maintain its beautiful finish. However, exposure to perfume, lotions, sweat, chemicals, chlorine and moisture over time can affect the appearance of any jewellery.

Is your jewellery suitable for sensitive skin?

I aim to offer pieces that are comfortable for everyday wear. Please refer to the material information provided on each product page before purchasing. If you have particularly sensitive skin or known metal allergies, I recommend checking the listed materials carefully.

How should I care for my Ziya & Co jewellery?

To keep your pieces looking their best:

  • Avoid direct contact with perfume, lotions and beauty products.
  • Remove jewellery before swimming, bathing or exercising.
  • Keep pieces away from chlorine, salt water and harsh chemicals.
  • Store your jewellery in a cool, dry place when you’re not wearing it.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing.

A little care goes a long way.

How should I store my jewellery?

I recommend storing each piece separately in its Ziya & Co packaging or jewellery pouch to prevent scratching, tangling and unnecessary exposure to moisture.
